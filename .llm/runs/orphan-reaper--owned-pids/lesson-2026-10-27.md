# Lesson — 2026-10-27

A global exclusion rule can preserve os/exec exit statuses while preventing the daemon's orphan collector from ever running. Test simultaneous lifetimes: keep an owned child live while another adopted child exits. A kernel test that exercises those cases only in sequence misses starvation.

Register ownership atomically with Start, collect only other positive PIDs, and let Wait release ownership after its pipe copies complete. Preserve the signal/backstop loop independently of app reads.

Negative controls must terminate after discovering the bug. The removed-start-fence mutation exposed a second receive in the test, turning a correct detection into a timeout. Retaining the received result produced a real, bounded red assertion.
